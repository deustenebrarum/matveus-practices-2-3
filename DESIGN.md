## Brand & Style

The visual identity embodies the oppressive solemnity, sacred militarism, and ancient craftsmanship of dark gothic sci-fi grimdark wargaming. It speaks directly to tabletop hobbyists, miniature painters, and collectors who view their armories not merely as commercial plastic, but as sanctified relics of eternal warfare. The experience evokes reverence, tactile authenticity, and dramatic gravitas—drawing from monastic cathedrals, armored sanctums, and weathered iron vaults.

The design movement blends **Tactile Skeuomorphism** and **Brutalist / Imperial Gothic Structure**:

- Deep, light-absorbing matte obsidian planes form the canvas.
- Antiqued gold, etched brass filigree, and wax purity seals frame vital interactive regions.
- Calligraphic and architectural serif typography is counterbalanced by sharp, utilitarian sans-serif for legible operational data.
- High-contrast metallic gradients, burnished bezels, and subtle aged parchment parchment accents provide tactile physical presence without compromising modern e-commerce scanning speed.

## Colors

The color palette draws from obsidian stone, sanctified metallic brass, aged parchment, and dried seal wax:

- **Primary (`#D4AF37`) — Reliquary Gold:** Represents divine authority, mastercrafted artificery, and golden imperial accents. Used for primary CTA buttons, elevated border frames, active tab indicators, and focal headlines.
- **Secondary (`#8A1C14`) — Purity Seal Red:** Evokes ritual wax seals, holy parchment ribbons, high-alert status counters, and destruction protocols. Used for cart count tags, warning badges, and destructive actions.
- **Tertiary (`#E6D7B8`) — Sacred Vellum / Parchment:** Provides an organic, ancient tactile accent for promo banners, narrative lore cards, and special offer certificates.
- **Neutral (`#0D0E12`) — Obsidian Abyss:** The foundational canvas. Paired with tiered surface tones:
  - Surface Void: `#0D0E12`
  - Surface Vault: `#14161D`
  - Surface Plinth: `#1A1D26`
  - Surface Inset / Input: `#0A0B0E`
  - Text Primary: `#EDE8DC` (Pale antique white)
  - Text Secondary: `#9A9FA8` (Weathered lead gray)
  - Border Dull Iron: `#2E3440`
  - Border Burnished Brass: `#8C733E`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs classical imperial authority with high-legibility combat logistics:

- **EB Garamond** serves as the heraldic voice. Used strictly in headlines, unit titles, faction insignia labels, and primary buttons in capitalized or small-caps styling. Generous tracking (`0.02em` to `0.08em`) enhances the monumentality of stone inscriptions and high gothic codexes.
- **Work Sans** provides crisp, objective utilitarian legibility for technical wargaming specifications: miniature scales (e.g., `SCALE: 32mm`), point costs, inventory tallies, filters, and prices.
- **Micro-Copy Rules:** All faction badges and filter section titles must be rendered in uppercase (`text-transform: uppercase`) with subtle letter-spacing to mirror engraved placards.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout follows an unyielding, structured architectural cadence mirroring armored vehicle plating and cathedral triptychs:

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column structured grid. Standard catalog interface uses a persistent 3-column filter ledger on the left (`280px` fixed or 3-column span) and a 9-column dynamic showcase for miniature cards (3 to 4 cards per row).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout. Filter ledger collapses into an overlay parchment drawer. Miniature showcase reflows to 2 columns with `1rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4-column layout. Single column or 2-column compact grid for unit cards. Outer margins shrink to `1rem` (`margin-mobile`), and padding within cards compresses to `space-sm` / `space-md` to preserve visual payload.
- **Vertical Spacing:** Section headings and reliquary groupings use `space-xl` gaps, while metadata pairs within cards retain tight structural pairing with `space-xs` and `space-sm`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on sterile cast drop-shadows; instead, it utilizes physical layering, metallic trim bevels, and atmospheric grimdark backlighting:

- **Level 0 (Abyssal Floor):** `#0D0E12` canvas base. Non-interactive backdrop.
- **Level 1 (Relic Slabs & Unit Cards):** `#14161D` fill bordered by a 1px composite outline of `#2E3440` with corner notched brackets or `#8C733E` burnished brass strokes. Internal top edge carries an inset hairline highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`).
- **Level 2 (Active Focus & Modal Shrines):** `#1A1D26` fill. Outlined with a dual-stroke aesthetic (outer `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)` and inner hairline). Accented with an ambient golden core haze: `0 0 24px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12), 0 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`.
- **Cart & Alert Overlays:** Deep charcoal modal backings with decorative ornate brass filigree borders and dark red inner glow accents (`0 0 16px rgba(138, 28, 20, 0.35)`).

## Shapes

The form language is disciplined, hard-edged, and monolithic. Sharp corners predominate, softened only by microscopic bevels:

- Base radius is restrained (`0.25rem` / `4px`), ensuring components preserve an armored, machined silhouette.
- **Corner Notches & Ornate Miters:** Card headers, modal frames, and primary purchase buttons employ 45-degree angled chamfers or decorative corner bracket accents to mirror fortified bulkheads and imperial architecture.
- Miniature circular badges (e.g., faction crests, wax stamps) are reserved for seals, skulls, and icon medallions, juxtaposed against rectilinear framing.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary Sanctified CTA ("Acquire Relic" / "Add to Cart"):**
  - Background: Linear gradient from `#C99726` to `#8C6518` with an etched inset border.
  - Text: `#0D0E12` or high-contrast crisp antique black, uppercase `EB Garamond` bold.
  - Hover state: Brightens with an inner golden ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.45)`) and slight gold gradient elevation.
  - Structure: Beveled ends or ornamental brackets at extreme left and right.
- **Secondary Ghost / Vault CTA:**
  - Background: `#14161D` with a 1px border of `#8C733E`.
  - Text: `#D4AF37`, transitioning to `#FFFFFF` on hover.

### Product & Miniature Cards

- Background: Surface Vault (`#14161D`) with 1px border `#3A3222` evolving to bright brass `#D4AF37` upon hover.
- Structure:
  - Miniature display viewport with dark radial gradient vignette behind miniature imagery.
  - Faction pill badge positioned top-left over image with hairline metallic rim.
  - Unit title rendered in `EB Garamond`, followed by technical scale stamp (e.g., `SCALE: 32mm`).
  - Bottom action deck featuring price in pale gold bold typography alongside an immediate ornate purchase button.

### Form Fields & Range Sliders

- Inputs: `#0A0B0E` background, 1px `#2E3440` perimeter border, focusing to `#D4AF37` with `0 0 6px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`.
- Placeholder: `#6A707E` italicized or uppercase muted sans-serif.
- Range Sliders: Rail rendered in dull iron `#242833`; active filled segment in burnished brass gradient; thumb shaped as an imperial diamond or notched shield.

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls

- Checkbox: Square 16px with beveled corners, `#0D0E12` center, `#4B4332` brass outline. Checked state reveals an engraved antique gold eagle/skull emblem or sharp crosshair mark with a `#D4AF37` solid fill.

### Badges & Faction Chips

- Faction Badges: Pill-shaped or chamfered miniature plaques with 1px golden outline, containing miniature heraldic sigils (Imperium skull, Chaos octagram, Ork glyph) alongside concise `label-sm` text.
- Purity Seal Discount Tag: Weathered parchment ribbon background (`#E6D7B8`) textured with jagged cut-edge ends, stamped with a crimson wax seal badge (`#8A1C14`) indicating special discount orders.

### Drawer Cart & Inspection Modals

- Fixed right-side slide-over and center modal frames encased in double-line brass framing (`#8C733E` outer, hairline gap, `#4A3B1C` inner).
- Header decorated with central Imperial heraldry wings and cross-skulls.
- Quantity counters: Segmented stepped inputs with brass `+` and `-` triggers flanking an obsidian counter cell.
